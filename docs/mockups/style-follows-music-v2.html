One road, three moods

Neon Highway is home base. When the song changes (a calm verse, the main groove, a big fast drop), the look changes with it, right on the beat, with a quick CRT glitch. The road, the lanes and the hit line never move, so your eyes don't have to re-learn anything mid-song. This preview runs a fake 18-second song on a loop.

How the game would decide (my guess, open to change)

While it builds the note chart, the game also splits the song into parts by energy: how loud it is and how busy the beat is. Quiet parts get the calm vector look, normal parts get neon, and the loudest, busiest parts get the 8-bit look. A sudden tempo change also triggers a switch. Menus and the song list always use neon.
