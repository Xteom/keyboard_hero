keyboard_hero: pick a look

The same game screen in three retro styles. Everything is drawn in code, so there are no images or fonts to download. All three show level 3, which has 4 lanes (4 keyboard sections). Click the one that feels right.

A · Neon Highway

80s synthwave style: a glowing road runs toward a striped sunset, notes rush at you in 3D, and everything has neon glow and CRT scanlines. This is the closest to a rock-concert feel.

B · 8-bit Arcade

A 1980s arcade cabinet look: chunky pixels, a small color palette, a flat playfield with notes dropping straight down, and score panels at the sides. It's the easiest to read at high speed.

C · Phosphor Vector

Like an old green-screen monitor or a vector arcade machine: everything is glowing green wireframe on black, with a 3D road and wireframe mountains. It's minimal, moody, and very light to run.

The same in every style

Notes rush toward a hit line. Each lane is one section of your keyboard. Score and combo sit at the top. Each hit flashes and gives a rating (PERFECT / GREAT / OK). When you miss a note, the music briefly goes muffled so you can hear the mistake.
